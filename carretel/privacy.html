Privacy Policy — Carretel

Last updated: November 9, 2026 · Versão em português

This policy explains how the Carretel app ("app"), published by BluYsh (individual developer Jean Jonas da Silva), handles your data. It follows Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Google Play policies.

1. Summary

  • Your notes, wheels, cards and review history stay only on your device. We have no server and never receive your content.
  • Anki files you import are read on your device and are not sent anywhere.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which may collect some device data to serve and measure ads.
  • The "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

2. Data stored on your device

Note text, cards (front and back), wheels, spaced-repetition dates and results, and app preferences are stored only in your device's internal storage. They are not sent to us or to third parties.

Anki import: when you pick an Anki deck file, the app reads it locally to create cards. The app only accesses the file you select and keeps no copy of it beyond the imported cards.

Export: if you export your data, the file is saved where you choose and is then under your control.

If Android automatic backup is turned on for your Google account, the system may include app data in your own account's backup. That backup is handled by Google according to your device settings, and we cannot access it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To do so, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process:

DataPurpose
Android advertising IDServing ads, frequency capping, measurement and, with your consent where required, ad personalization
IP address (used to estimate approximate location)Choosing region-appropriate ads and preventing fraud
Device and app information (model, OS, language, version)Delivering compatible ads and diagnosing errors
Ad interactions (views and taps)Performance measurement and fraud prevention

Google processes this data as an independent controller. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices:

  • For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and wherever else the law requires, the app shows a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice later in the app settings.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Privacy → Ads (the path may vary by manufacturer).
  • Buying "Remove ads" stops the app from loading ads.

Legal basis (GDPR): personalized ads rely on your consent; non-personalized ads, measurement and fraud prevention rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app free and safe.

4. "Remove ads" purchase

The purchase uses Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment and only sends the app a purchase confirmation (a purchase identifier), so the app can turn off ads and restore the purchase if you reinstall. We do not receive your name, email, address or payment details.

5. Android permissions

The app uses internet access (to load ads and verify purchases), vibration (for haptic feedback when spinning the wheel) and the technical permissions added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google Play Billing. None of them asks you for authorization. File access happens only through the Android file picker, when you choose a file to import or a place to export. The app does not access your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell or share personal data. The only third party involved is Google (AdMob and Google Play), for the purposes above. Data processed by Google may be transferred outside your country under the safeguards described in Google's policy.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children's data. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will help.

8. Your rights (LGPD and GDPR)

You may request confirmation of processing, access, correction, deletion, portability, information about sharing, objection and withdrawal of consent. Because your content stays only on your device, you can export or delete it at any time (by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings). For data processed by Google, use your Google Account tools. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9. Security

Your data is protected by Android's own security (app-private storage). Communication with Google services uses encrypted connections.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. Relevant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
